ErdosProblems.MulticolourTriangleRamsey.divergentRamseyRoot

IdeaTo prove $r(k)^{1/k}\to\infty$, fix any real $b$ and let $m=\max\{3,\lceil b e^{38}\rceil\}$. For all $k\ge C(m)$ the palette colour count is large enough ($C(3)\le k$) to apply the sharp exponential lower bound, which yields a stage $H$ with $C(H)\le k<C(H+1)$ and $(H/e^{38})^{k}\le r(k)$. Since $k<C(H+1)$, monotonicity of $C$ forces $H\ge m$, hence $b\le b\,e^{38}\le \lceil b\,e^{38}\rceil\le m\le H$, i.e. $b\le H/e^{38}$. Taking $k$-th roots of $(H/e^{38})^{k}\le r(k)$ (the real-power equivalence is valid because $k>0$) gives $b\le H/e^{38}\le r(k)^{1/k}$, which is the required eventual lower bound.

ErdosProblems.MulticolourTriangleRamsey.allColourPaletteRamsey_exponential_bound_sharp

IdeaThe proof picks the stage $H$ at which the palette colour count crosses $k$: $C(H)\le k<C(H+1)$, with $H\ge 2$ from the bracketing lemma and $H\ge 3$ because $k\ge C(3)$. The crossing inequality is converted into the ratio bound $L(H)k\le (L(H)+34)C(H)$ by multiplying by $L(H)$ and applying the adjacent scaled estimate. That ratio bound is exactly what the transfer lemma needs to pass from exponent $k$ and base $e^{-38}$ to exponent $C(H)$ and base $e^{-4}$, i.e. $(H/e^{38})^k\le (H/e^{4})^{C(H)}$; the recursive palette Ramsey bound then bounds the right side by $r(C(H))$, and monotonicity of $r$ in the number of colours replaces $C(H)$ by $k$. This shows the exponential bound is sharp at every $k$ at or above $C(3)$.
